**Geometry and evidence limits**

`tests/output/codex-ux-audit/evidence/collect-vi-en.jsonl` and `tests/output/codex-ux-audit/evidence/collect-first-dark-dpi.jsonl` record Options at **1104 × 822 px**, for both User Data and HTML Compatibility in vi/en and the dark-requested run. That is 54 px taller than the requested 768-pixel comparison height. However, `upstream/libreoffice/cui/source/options/treeopt.cxx` sizes its content from 30 tree rows and caps that height at 80% of the current display. These 1600 × 1000 captures therefore do **not** prove an Options overflow on an actual 1366 × 768 display. Record the measured size, but do not promote it to a small-screen defect without that capture.

The other oversized dialog in the records is the already-known **1350 × 815 px NĐ 30 wizard**. The current source and installed `wizard_nd30.py` both specify **600 × 300 AppFont**, and `fix-nd30-compact.png` shows the compact layout consistent with the coordinator's 675-pixel measurement. The **1486 × 901 px** `w-close-query` records identify the Writer document window, not the save-confirmation dialog; they are not oversized-dialog findings.

All six DPI-144 captures were inspected. No additional text overlap or clipping is established beyond the excluded known items; the ribbon exposes overflow arrows. This does not verify the controls inside those overflow menus. The `vi-dark-*` captures retain light GTK surfaces and cannot establish dark-mode contrast. `vi-w-welcome.png`, `en-w-welcome.png`, `vi-dark-w-welcome.png`, and both print-preview captures show the editor rather than the named dialog/mode, so they do not establish those surfaces' behavior. The first-launch welcome itself is visible and readable in `vi-first-first-launch.png`.

Excluded the supplied owner decisions, known Start Center tab label, style-gallery preview count, missing-font warning, no-window-manager placement/stacking, and documented harness failures. The Vietnamese update result in `en-w-update.png` is also excluded: `docs/UPDATE_CHECK.md` and `extensions/hst-vietnamese-tools/python/hst_office/update_ui.py` explicitly classify that content as Vietnamese system/intro text. Current update **menu** captions are English in en-US, as required.
